How this works

Every egg, skull and data module placed in the game has a unique save ID. When you pick one up, the game records that ID in World0.sav. This page searches your save for all 434 IDs; any ID that isn't recorded is an item you haven't collected. The maps were drawn from the game's own level geometry: only floors and ledges are shown, coloured by height, and where floors overlap the higher one is drawn on top. Map "north" follows the level editor's top view, which may not match the in-game camera. Area borders were grouped by hand and may not match the game's area names exactly.

Totals: 50 Orbewa Eggs (hand in to Erma), 69 Silver Skulls (skull door under the Petrified Garden), 10 Data Modules (hand in to Ossi), 3 Jigeki Keys, 104 chests, 105 secret doors (not counting the 10 data-module walls) and 93 meals.

Chest contents come from the level data; chests marked ★ hold something other than consumables (a weapon, throwable, accessory, key item or treasure). Meal tickets: the save and level files don't record which meals hide a ticket, so every meal is shown and marked eaten or not; the save's own "meal tickets collected" counter is shown on the progress card.
