How this works

Every egg, skull and data module placed in the game has a unique save ID. When you pick one up, the game records that ID in World0.sav. This page searches your save for all 129 IDs; any ID that isn't recorded is an item you haven't collected. The maps were drawn from the game's own level geometry: only floors and ledges are shown, coloured by height, and where floors overlap the higher one is drawn on top. Map "north" follows the level editor's top view, which may not match the in-game camera. Area borders were grouped by hand and may not match the game's area names exactly.

Totals: 50 Orbewa Eggs (hand in to Erma), 69 Silver Skulls (skull door under the Petrified Garden), 10 Data Modules (hand in to Ossi).
